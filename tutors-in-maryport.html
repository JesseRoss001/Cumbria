Questions about tutoring in Maryport

Do you send tutors to homes in Maryport?

Cumbria Tutoring focuses on online tutoring. This keeps the tutor pool wider and avoids being limited by local travel distance.

Can my child have GCSE Maths or Science support?

Yes. We can route enquiries for GCSE Maths, GCSE English, GCSE Science, Combined Science and individual Science subjects.

What should I send before the first lesson?

Send the year group, subject, exam board if known, recent results, target grade, weak topics and preferred weekly times.
